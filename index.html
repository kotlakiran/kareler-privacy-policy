Kareler Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2024

Kareler respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use the Kareler mobile application.

Information Collection and Use

Kareler does not collect, store, transmit, or process personally identifiable information.

Game progress, profile statistics, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore.

Permissions

VIBRATE: Used only to provide haptic feedback while drawing lines or claiming boxes. Haptic feedback can be disabled in the app settings.
